# HubSpot CRM & Customer Support Operations Portfolio

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository showcases my hands-on practice using **HubSpot CRM** to manage customer records, sales opportunities, customer support tickets, follow-ups, CRM data quality, communication, reporting, and business operations.

The portfolio was built as a practical learning environment using **fictional customer and company data**. It demonstrates how CRM information can be organized and maintained throughout a customer journey.

# 🏆 End-to-End CRM Capstone

## Mwangi Business Solutions

Completed a simulated end-to-end customer journey using fictional CRM data.

### Customer

**Daniel Mwangi**  
Operations Director  
**Mwangi Business Solutions**

### Journey

**Contact → Company → Deal → Customer → Support Ticket → Resolution**

The capstone included:

1. Creating the company
2. Creating the contact
3. Associating the contact with the company
4. Creating a $2,000 VA Operations Support deal
5. Progressing the deal through Inquiry Received → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Closed Won
6. Recording sales activities
7. Creating follow-up tasks
8. Creating a customer support ticket
9. Classifying the ticket as Account Access
10. Setting ticket priority
11. Progressing the ticket from New to In Progress
12. Resolving the support issue
13. Closing the ticket
14. Completing the follow-up task

### Capstone Objective

Demonstrate the ability to manage a fictional client journey across CRM, sales operations, customer support, follow-up activities, and data management.
